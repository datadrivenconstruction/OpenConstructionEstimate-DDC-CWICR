# Provenance and license: China (定额 (预算定额))

## Source

- System: Construction consumption quotas (Dinge), valued under GB 50500 (定额 (预算定额)).
- Issuer and edition: Beijing municipal construction quota (2012).
- Region id in the platform: `ZH_CHINA`. Currency: CNY. Language: Chinese.
- Size: 11,312 work items, 61,007 resource lines. Money: unit prices in CNY.

## Legal basis of the source

Official government construction quotas, publicly issued by the municipal authorities. Government norms of this kind are public technical documents.

## What DDC adds and licenses

The underlying norms and official prices are public facts. What DDC compiled and owns is
the value-added layer: the harmonised 95-column schema, the English and multilingual
translations, the World Bank PPP repricing for 48 markets, and the cross-classification.
This compilation is released under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0
(CC BY-NC 4.0), with a separate DDC commercial license for commercial use.

This is possible because the source is not under a ShareAlike or copyleft license. A
plain public or Attribution source lets an adapter place a more restrictive license on
its own derivative layer while the source stays available under its own terms directly
from the issuer. The NonCommercial term applies to the DDC compilation, not to the
underlying public facts, which anyone may still obtain from the issuer.
